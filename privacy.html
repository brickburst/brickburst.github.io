This policy explains what information the Brick Burst app ("the app") for Android handles, and why. The app is made by DEVELOPER_NAME ("we"). If you have questions, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves your best score, the number of games played, your game in progress, and your settings (sound, vibration, tutorial progress) on your device only. This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to continue a game. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks), and diagnostics to prevent fraud.

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch the app asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the in-game menu under Privacy settings. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads depending on your device).
  • US state privacy rights: where applicable, Google provides the choices required by US state privacy laws through the same consent and ad settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

We do not run servers that receive your data. Data held by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
